## Why this release exists

Live validation on the acquired BUY thesis reached a clean finalized runway
(27.53 versus 10.00 required) but the final plan remained WATCH_ONLY with only the
generic reason ANALYSIS_HISTORY_WINDOW_INCOMPLETE.

The history separation gate treated extended DXY depth as the same kind of execution
authority evidence as XAU D1/H4/H1/M15 even though the Master Sniper contract makes
DXY confirmation/confluence only.

## 6.5.97 policy

- XAU D1 >= 1 year, H4 >= 4 months, H1 >= 4 weeks and M15 >= 3 trading days
  remain HARD execution-history gates.
- Live prompt minimum counts remain HARD for every required XAU and DXY series:
  XAU D1/H4/H1/M15 = 80/120/160/160 and DXY D1/H4/H1 = 60/80/100.
- Extended DXY D1/H4/H1 depth is now confluence-quality telemetry. Short extended
  depth by itself cannot manufacture, move or veto an XAU institutional zone.
- History span calculation uses max(timestamp)-min(timestamp), so payload ordering
  cannot create a false zero-span failure.
- Finalized plan text now exports:
  history_window_failures,
  history_confluence_warnings,
  history_window_metrics.
- The dashboard renders the exact failed history legs and the received bar/depth
  telemetry when a history hold remains.

No spread, stale-snapshot, news, source-exact geometry, liquidity, M15 invalidation,
M1 confirmation, target-direction, minimum-RR, no-chase, parity or risk rule is
weakened.
