## Live validation defect

The active countertrend BUY thesis could have a valid acquired owner and open TP2/TP3
while TP1 was already behind the ownership anchor. The final professional separation
gate still measured runway to the frozen zone TP1. For the validated
`PZ_H4H1_BUY_6` example this measured 9.21 from the tactical core to 4285 and
incorrectly forced `WATCH_ONLY`, even though the next open owner objective was
4303.32 and provided sufficient runway.

The dashboard compounded the problem by calling any positive `zone.clear_run` a pass
and by displaying authority from the pre-finalization analysis policy rather than the
exact finalized MT5 plan.

## 6.5.96 / 3.45 behavior

1. Final execution separation uses the finalized plan's `next_open_target` first,
   falling back to `original_target1` only when no active objective is available.
2. The countertrend runway requirement remains 10.0 XAU price units. No threshold is
   reduced.
3. The dashboard reads `ea_mode`, execution authority, separation reason and runway
   telemetry from the same finalized plan text delivered to Sequence.
4. A legitimate `WATCH_ONLY` plan is displayed as such instead of being mislabeled as
   an authority mismatch.
5. Sequence 3.45 decides active-owner priority from the persisted owner mirror before
   accepted-flip arming/evaluation can mutate runtime authority. A dormant flip remains
   persisted for audit/expiry but cannot supersede a matching non-terminal owner.
6. Spread, stale snapshot, news, parity, target-direction, minimum-RR, M1 confirmation,
   no-chase and risk guards are unchanged.

This release does not authorize an entry by itself. It reconciles thesis lifecycle,
final plan truth and MT5 runtime authority.
