## Live verification finding

Cloud 6.5.107 deployed correctly, but the live 29 September state exposed one
over-conservative eligibility check.

Observed state:
- active acquired BUY thesis
- ownership anchor about 4148.93
- frozen BUY objective about 4154.04
- current market about 4144.18 / 4144.34
- newly qualified SELL primary starting at 4148.78
- canonical BUY front-run objective about 4148.42
- Sequence positions = 0

The new 4148.42 lifecycle cap was correctly in front of the live market and in
front of the opposing SELL zone, but it was below the old 4148.93 ownership
anchor. Cloud 6.5.107 required every cap to remain profitable relative to that
historical anchor, so reconciliation was rejected and the dashboard continued to
show 4154.04.

That requirement was wrong for a flat owner. The ownership anchor is historical
handoff/audit truth. When there are zero open positions, no live trade exists
whose profit must be measured from that old anchor.

## 6.5.108 behavior

For an acquired thesis with fresh Sequence truth proving zero open positions:

1. The opposing-zone cap still comes from the canonical Master Sniper proximal
   front-run calculation.
2. It still must tighten the frozen deepest objective; it can never move the
   lifecycle destination farther away.
3. It must be ahead of the current executable quote by the normal spread-aware
   target gap:
   - BUY: cap above current ask plus safety gap.
   - SELL: cap below current bid minus safety gap.
4. It no longer has to be beyond the historical ownership anchor.
5. The historical anchor, original target ladder and exact frozen owner payload
   remain unchanged for audit.

For the reproduced live geometry, 4148.42 is therefore a valid effective BUY
lifecycle destination even though it is below the historical 4148.93 ownership
anchor, because it remains safely above the current BUY executable quote.

## Safety remains unchanged

- Reconciliation still requires fresh Sequence positions = 0.
- If a position is open, the cap is deferred; no live TP is modified.
- The cap cannot be completed by price history that predates cap creation.
- Reaching the cap releases/completes the owner thesis only.
- The opposing SELL receives no automatic entry or authority.
- Fresh M1 location and the full sweep -> MSS/BOS -> displacement -> dealing
  range -> OTE/PD-array sequence remain mandatory.
- M15 invalidation, grade, risk, spread, news and runway rules are unchanged.

A production-shaped regression reproduces the 4148.93 / 4144.18-4144.34 /
4148.78 / 4154.04 geometry and requires the effective next owner objective to
become 4148.42 while the frozen 4154.04 target remains persisted as audit truth.

No MT5 reinstall or reattach is required.
