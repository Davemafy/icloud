# Trade Zone 6.3.33 — Safety / Plan Synchronization

Status: DEMO / PAPER ONLY

- DataBridge: 1.52
- Sequence EA: 3.43
- Cloud companion: 6.5.94

## Purpose

A Cloud safety block (spread, stale snapshot, or protected news window) must suspend orders without erasing the selected institutional plan, execution authority, owner mirror, risk context, or Master Sniper parity fingerprint.

Sequence 3.42 returned before parsing the plan whenever `live_block=1`, so the dashboard could display `PLAN_REFRESH_FAILED` / `SNIPER CONTRACT UNVERIFIED` even though the real reason was simply a spread or other safety hold.

Sequence 3.43 always parses and synchronizes the plan first, then enforces the Cloud live block inside the order safety guards. No safety threshold is weakened.

Cloud 6.5.94 also permits a distinct Level-2 institutional zone to share a thin envelope edge with Level-1 (maximum 20% of the smaller band) and chooses the nearest qualified reserve before a more remote reserve. Reserves remain context-only until fresh requalification/promotion.
