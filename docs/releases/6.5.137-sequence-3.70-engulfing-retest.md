## Execution refinement

A valid recent-zone Model-2 directional M1 engulfing remains the fastest direct trigger, but Sequence 3.70 no longer treats an older engulfing as a direct market entry several bars later.

If the fresh engulfing entry is missed, the EA may preserve the engulfed opposite-candle body as a causal value band for a bounded M1 retest.

A recovery entry requires:

- the original engulfing to have valid recent institutional-zone context;
- a revisit of the engulfed-body value band within the configured bounded window;
- a fresh CLOSED M1 directional rejection/continuation candle;
- the confirmation close to remain near value rather than chase an extended move;
- the current HTF thesis/authority and objective lifecycle to remain valid;
- the protected M1 swing SL from Sequence 3.69;
- minimum RR, spread/snapshot, parity and broker constraints to remain valid.

## Campaign discipline

This is not R3.

The recovery path consumes the existing current P0/R1/R2 slot. It does not increase MaxReentriesPerThesis and does not reset a campaign after losses or re-ranking.

Therefore an already exhausted campaign remains blocked. The refinement is for future cases where the direct engulfing was genuinely missed or where an allowed R1/R2 slot still exists.
