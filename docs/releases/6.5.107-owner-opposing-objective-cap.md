# Cloud 6.5.107 — Owner objective reconciliation at a new opposing primary

Status: DEMO / PAPER ONLY

MT5 stable package is unchanged:
- Stable 6.3.38
- DataBridge 1.55
- Sequence EA 3.45

## Live finding

Cloud 6.5.106 correctly preserved the acquired BUY thesis while requiring a fresh
M1 entry location, but live re-analysis exposed a separate lifecycle conflict.

The acquired BUY thesis had frozen its earlier objective at approximately
4154.04. A newly qualified active SELL primary then formed below that old
objective, with the current Master Sniper liquidity policy correctly front-running
that opposing SELL zone at approximately 4148.44.

Without reconciliation, the system could therefore show all of these truths at
the same time:

- BUY thesis owner still active
- current BUY objective policy capped in front of the SELL zone
- frozen owner objective still deeper inside the active SELL core
- opposite SELL execution still blocked by the BUY ownership lock

That could leave a completed directional idea locked longer than the current
institutional map justified.

## 6.5.107 behavior

An already-acquired thesis may now receive a one-way lifecycle objective cap when
all of the following are true:

1. A genuinely qualified active opposing primary exists.
2. The canonical opposing-zone front-run price is closer than the owner's frozen
   deepest live objective.
3. The cap remains on the profit side of the original ownership anchor.
4. A fresh Sequence heartbeat proves there are zero open positions.

When those conditions are met:

- the original owner target ladder remains frozen audit truth
- the frozen ownership-zone payload is not rewritten
- the new cap is persisted separately with the opposing zone id and timestamp
- target revalidation uses the cap as the effective remaining destination
- MT5 owner-mirror target fields project the effective capped ladder while the
  exact frozen owner payload remains unchanged
- the cap can only tighten the lifecycle; it never moves the target farther away
- a later absent or more distant opposing zone does not silently relax the cap
- fresh same-direction M1 confirmation remains mandatory for any new entry

If Sequence reports an open position, or position truth is stale/unavailable,
cap reconciliation is deferred. The Cloud does not change an existing live
position's protected target merely because a new opposite zone appeared.

## Completion truth

The cap cannot be completed retroactively by a favourable excursion that happened
before the cap existed.

Only evidence at or after the cap-set timestamp can complete it:

- current executable quote beyond the cap, or
- an M15 bar that began after the cap was established and traded through it.

Once the effective cap is reached, the owner lifecycle becomes
OBJECTIVE_COMPLETE using:

OPPOSING_ZONE_OWNER_OBJECTIVE_CAP_REACHED

Normal M15 accepted invalidation remains unchanged.

## Safety boundary

This release does not:

- manufacture a new BUY or SELL zone
- grant the opposing side an instant entry
- bypass the full M1 sequence
- loosen grade, risk, spread, news, or runway guards
- change open-position targets during reconciliation
- erase the original owner target history
- use pre-cap price history to claim a cap completion

After owner completion/release, an opposing zone still needs its own valid
location and fresh M1 confirmation before Sequence can enter.

No MT5 reinstall or reattach is required.
