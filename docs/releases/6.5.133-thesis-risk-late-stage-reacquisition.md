# Cloud 6.5.133 — 0.30% thesis cap and late-stage HTF reacquisition

## Scope

- Cloud: 6.5.133
- MT5 stable package: unchanged at 6.4.7
- DataBridge: unchanged at 1.58
- Sequence EA: unchanged at 3.67
- DEMO / PAPER ONLY

## Risk contract

The Cloud now publishes a code-authoritative maximum thesis/campaign risk of 0.30% of the non-compounding validation sizing base.

- TREND A+ = 0.300%
- TREND A = 0.225%
- COUNTERTREND A+ = 0.150%
- COUNTERTREND A = 0.075%
- B+ = 0.100%

The existing campaign allocation remains P0 60% / R1 30% / R2 10%. Model-specific and flip multipliers may only reduce risk. Cloud plan values are fingerprinted and revalidated by Sequence before order submission.

## Late-stage owner execution

After two owner objectives have completed, historical thesis ownership remains lifecycle/audit truth but is no longer sufficient by itself to authorize a fresh entry.

A new same-direction entry requires the CURRENT freshly ranked HTF zone to be active/execution-grade and either:

- live-interacting with price, or
- already carrying a valid M1_READY handoff.

The frozen owner is not transferred to the new zone. Opposite-side execution remains blocked until normal owner release. Fresh M1 confirmation remains mandatory.

## Dashboard truth

The journal/checklist now exposes whether late-stage current-HTF reacquisition is required and whether it is satisfied. Risk percentages display to three decimals so 0.075% and 0.225% are not rounded misleadingly.

## Release boundary

This is a Cloud-only release. The MT5 stable package is intentionally unchanged because Sequence 3.67 already consumes the signed/fingerprinted Cloud risk percentages and fails closed on plan/parity mismatch.
