## Primary first-entry contract

The primary first entry is now intentionally simple:

1. Price contacts the published premium/discount institutional envelope.
2. M1 takes local liquidity.
3. M1 closes through the nearest internal 1-minute swing: **micro MSS**.
4. Price pulls back.
5. The next closed M1 candle closes in the trade direction.
6. Sequence may send the DEMO/PAPER entry if spread/snapshot/risk/target/RR safety still passes.

MSS is **M1 micro structure only**. It is not an M5, M15, H1 or H4 structure-break requirement.

M15 validates zone health and accepted invalidation only. A separate M15 sweep/reclaim is not required to arm the primary M1 search.

The primary first entry no longer requires tactical-core contact, ATR displacement, a new dealing range, OTE, FVG, PD-array overlap, or a fixed-dollar runway gate.

## Stop and reward logic

The initial stop remains beyond the institutional zone with the existing buffer. Sequence sizes risk from the actual entry to that buffered stop.

Absolute runway is observation telemetry only. Entry quality is checked with minimum RR from the actual M1 entry and buffered zone-distal stop to still-open directional objectives. Objectives already behind the actual entry are removed before order targets are built.

## No-chase rule

The pullback must immediately precede the final closed M1 directional confirmation candle. A historical sweep/MSS followed by an extended move cannot be reused as a late primary entry.

## Scope

This simplification applies to the **primary first entry**. Existing accepted-zone flip, liquidity-reversal, re-entry, position-management, spread/news/snapshot safety and thesis-ownership controls remain separate.
